Support

Need help with Bunny Bounce? Email gothriveforge@gmail.com. Tell us what happened and which phone or tablet you're using.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between devices on its own.

Will I lose my bunnies if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes the progress saved on that device.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and choose Reset all progress. This erases your bunnies, nursery babies, food counts and Bunny Bucks. Your sound and music choices stay as they are.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open Settings, where Sound effects and Music each turn on and off.
