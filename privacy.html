Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The Bunny Bounce app does not collect, store or share any personal data. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android apps.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to any server run by Bunny Bounce.

Your saved game

Your progress, such as your bunnies, nursery babies and settings, is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us, and we can't see it.

To erase it, use Reset all progress in Settings, or delete the app.

Children

Bunny Bounce is an all-ages game. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no personal information from children under 13.

Apple and Google

You download the app from the App Store or Google Play. Those stores run under their own privacy policies, and they may give developers general statistics such as download counts. Those statistics don't identify you.

This website

This website has no ads, analytics or cookies of its own. The company that hosts it may log visitors' technical details, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
